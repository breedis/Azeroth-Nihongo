## Description

**Learn Japanese one word at a time, from the things you already look at in the game.**

Hover over a monster, NPC, item or spell and a small box next to the tooltip shows its name in Japanese:

> 狼 おおかみ [ookami] = wolf

The game stays in English, and you pick up about 1,900 words for the creatures, items, food, materials, places and spells of Azeroth.

### Features
- **Kanji, kana, romaji and English.** Each part can be shown or hidden.
- **Kanji breakdown.** See which kana each kanji is read as: 宿(やど) 屋(や).
- **Words with more than one meaning** are told apart by context. *Light Leather* gives 軽い, *Holy Light* gives 光.
- **Quiz.** Practise dictionary words, words from random items or from your bags, or kana. Words you get wrong come back more often.
- **Kana quiz.** Hiragana, katakana or both. Wrong answers include look-alikes such as ぬ/め and シ/ツ.
- **Pop quiz.** The quiz opens by itself on a timer, after quest turn-ins or on level up. It never opens in combat, and you can keep it out of dungeons and raids.
- **Lexicon.**
  - A kana chart.
  - Every kanji with its readings.
  - Your words sorted into right, wrong and undiscovered, with practice for any list.
- **Writing practice.** Draw kana and kanji with the mouse, stroke by stroke, like a practice sheet. Each stroke is checked for its place, shape, direction and order. Trace, hints only or from memory.
- **Untranslated words** you meet are saved, so the dictionary can grow.
- **Pronunciation** with the Japanese text-to-speech voice in Windows, if one is installed.

### Getting started
- Click the **JP** button to turn it on or off.
- Right-click it for the quiz.
- Alt-click it for the lexicon.
- Type `/jp help` to see every command.

If kanji show as boxes, quit the game completely and start it again. A new font is only loaded on a full restart.

### Pronunciation
To add a Japanese voice in Windows:
1. Go to Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices → Japanese.
2. Restart WoW.
